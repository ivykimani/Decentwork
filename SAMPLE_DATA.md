## Worker Rights (6 categories)

1. **Safe Working Conditions** (Safety)
   - Proper safety equipment
   - Regular safety training
   - Right to refuse unsafe work
   - OSHA compliance

2. **Fair Wages** (Compensation)
   - Minimum wage requirements
   - Overtime pay (1.5x)
   - Equal pay for equal work
   - Timely payment

3. **Reasonable Working Hours** (Working Hours)
   - 40 hours per week standard
   - Overtime compensation
   - Required rest breaks
   - Limits on consecutive work days

4. **Freedom of Association** (Rights)
   - Right to organize unions
   - Protection from retaliation
   - Collective bargaining
   - Right to strike

5. **Non-Discrimination** (Equality)
   - Protection in hiring
   - Equal treatment in promotions
   - Reasonable accommodations
   - Harassment protection

6. **Family and Medical Leave** (Rights)
   - Up to 12 weeks unpaid leave
   - Job protection
   - Continued health insurance
   - Leave for family events
